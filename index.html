How it works: the app tracks how fast the texture inside the box slides across the frame, then converts pixels to metres using the distance above. Point the camera at the ground (or out of a side window at a nearby wall or road edge), keep it steady, and keep the distance setting accurate. For better accuracy, move at a steady speed with GPS on and tap Calibrate. It is an estimate, not a certified speedometer. Very fast motion or featureless surfaces will read low.
